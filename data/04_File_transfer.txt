SMB Share Create # 1:icacls $PWD /grant 'Everyone:(OI)(CI)RX' /T | Out-Null; New-SmbShare -Name 'MyShare' -Path $PWD -ReadAccess 'Everyone' # Create SMB share on Windows target; target-machine
